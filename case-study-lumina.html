01 THE CONTEXT & PROBLEM STATEMENT

OVERCOMING IMMIGRATION PORTAL ANXIETY

Visa application portals are notorious for poor usability: endless dense multi-page forms, confusing legal jargon, ambiguous document dimensions, and zero saving mechanisms. First-time international travelers frequently experience intense anxiety that a minor paperwork error will void their flight tickets.

⚠
[USABILITY AUDIT // COGNITIVE OVERLOAD]

The Bureaucracy Trap: 62% Abandonment at Document Upload

Benchmarking of legacy portals revealed that users spent 30+ minutes filling out personal details only to encounter a cryptic file upload requirement (e.g. "Passport photo must be exactly 35x45mm, under 200kb, JPEG 2000"). Facing rejection without guidance, over 60% of applicants gave up.

02 RESEARCH & USER INSIGHTS

CORE INSIGHTS FROM USABILITY TESTING

[01] FORM FATIGUE

One Question at a Time

Breaking down a 40-question government form into progressive, conversational cards reduced cognitive stress and kept user focus sharp without visual distractions.

IMPACT: 2.5x faster data entry speed
[02] FEEDBACK VISIBILITY

Automated Document Pre-Flight

Real-time OCR passport boundary detection informs the user immediately whether their uploaded scan is clear, eliminating weeks of embassy rejection anxiety.

IMPACT: 99.4% first-time document acceptance
