01 THE CONTEXT & PROBLEM STATEMENT

THE FRAGMENTATION OF INDONESIA'S CULTURAL HERITAGE

Indonesia possesses over 300 distinct ethnic groups and tens of thousands of historical landmarks, batik ateliers, and puppet theater stages. However, information about these treasures is scattered across outdated government PDF brochures, unindexed Instagram stories, and conflicting Google Maps pins.

⚠
[AUDIT REPORT // CULTURAL VISIBILITY GAP]

Discovery Failure: Why Urban Explorers Miss Out on Grassroots Heritage

Surveys showed that 78% of young urban explorers wanted to attend traditional cultural performances but had no reliable way to verify actual showtimes or ticketing. Artisans and independent historical societies suffered from empty seats simply because their events were invisible to modern digital search channels.

ISSUE 01: Unverified Operating Hours
ISSUE 02: Fragmented Ticketing Systems
ISSUE 03: Obsolete Wayfinding Info
02 RESEARCH & USER INSIGHTS

SYNTHESIZING FIELD INSIGHTS FROM EXPLORERS & VENUE HOSTS

We conducted contextual interviews across 20 cultural institutions and 45 active event-goers in Yogyakarta and Surakarta to identify the exact friction points preventing regular attendance.

[01] EXPLORER INTENT

The "Is It Open?" Anxiety

Visitors frequently traveled 1+ hours only to discover a heritage museum closed for preservation without public notice. Real-time verified status badges were considered non-negotiable.

SOLUTION: Verified venue verification badge
[02] COMMUNITY HUB

Social Validation Deficit

Younger demographics rely on community-submitted photos, authentic visitor tips, and crowd levels before committing their weekends to an unfamiliar district.

SOLUTION: Community check-in & review matrix
[03] CURATOR DESK

Artisan Publishing Barrier

Elderly cultural caretakers cannot manage complex ticketing CMS engines. They needed a dead-simple form taking under 90 seconds to publish upcoming stage dates.

SOLUTION: Micro-CMS curator interface

04 RETROSPECTIVE & KEY TAKEAWAYS

CRAFTING DIGITAL SPACES FOR VERNACULAR HERITAGE

WHAT WORKED

  • 1. Geo-located Visual Feed: Users discovered landmarks within 2km of their daily commute they previously never knew existed.
  • 2. High-Contrast Neo-Brutalism: The bold typography gave Indonesian traditional arts a modern, hype, and culturally resonant editorial flair.

WHAT TO IMPROVE

  • 1. Offline Audio Guides: Many remote historical temple complexes have zero cellular reception; offline audio tour files are planned for v3.
  • 2. Native Language Support: Adding Javanese and Sundanese script toggles will strengthen local community ownership.
