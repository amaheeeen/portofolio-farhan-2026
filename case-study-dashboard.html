01 THE PROBLEM & ERGONOMIC CHALLENGE

THE HYPER-COLORFUL TRADING COCKPIT TRAP

Modern fintech trading platforms resemble pinball machines: bright rainbow charts, flashing red and green tickers, and dozens of competing notifications. In high-stress markets, this chromatic overload triggers severe decision fatigue, causing traders to miss genuine anomalies.

Design Philosophy: When Everything Shouts, Nothing is Heard

Mono Stock establishes a rigorous monochromatic foundation. Grayscale grids and neutral typography form 95% of the interface. Only critical market breakouts utilize single-color accents, ensuring that when color speaks, the user reacts immediately.
